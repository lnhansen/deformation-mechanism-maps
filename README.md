# Deformation Mechanism Maps

Interactive deformation mechanism maps for olivine, built for the Rock and Mineral Physics Laboratory, University of Minnesota. Single-file web app (`index.html`), no dependencies.

Flow laws: Hirth & Kohlstedt (2003), Hansen et al. (2011), Mei et al. (2010), Yabe & Hiraga (2020) grain-boundary diffusion controlled and interface-reaction controlled creep, and the Breithaupt et al. (2023) dislocation model.

The default scenarios reproduce Figures 22 and 23 of the 2026 Treatise on Geophysics chapter (Hansen, Kohlstedt & Hiraga): same flow laws, field names, colour scheme (diffusion creep in blues, dislocation creep in greens), solidus (1350 °C), and the Karato et al. (1980) and Meyers & Kohlstedt (2026) piezometers. Temperatures are in °C. All parameters editable; any power law or Peierls law can be added.
